// mission

A business computing ecosystem built on transparency

The proprietary software cycle demands you keep buying hardware and licenses to stay supported, but it doesn't have to be that way. The open-source community has developed tools that can work for you on hardware you already own.

The Problem

Each new Windows release renders functional hardware obsolete. Businesses discard perfectly capable machines, pay escalating licensing fees, and lose control over their own data and workflows without the expertise or support to migrate.

Our Approach

We audit your current environment, identify compatible open-source replacements, and manage your migration. Any profits we make get redirected into funding development of the open-source tools your business relies on.

Who We Serve

Small and medium businesses, nonprofits, schools, and municipalities - any organisation whose daily computing needs are browsers, documents, spreadsheets, email, and line-of-business applications. If you find yourself irked by Windows-specific software, odds are we can find a solution to help you.

Why Now

Windows 11's hardware requirements already forced one wave of early retirement of perfectly good hardware. Let's not do that again.

Windows is already putting adds and telemetry directly into your operating system, and continues to push your data into their proprietary cloud. We believe that is unnecessary, anti-consumer and a threat to the digital privacy of both you and your business.

We are here to help you take ownership of your business devices. Linux solutions underpin the majority of the computing world, increasingly linux shows that it can serve the interests of business users, administrators and owners in addition to developers and hobbyists. Let's take the step to linux so that you can own your data, have full transparency and observability on all of your business machines, and make the most of hardware that you already own.

// software we work with

Proven open-source tools

Every project we recommend is actively maintained, freely auditable, and backed by a community or foundation with long-term stability.

GNU Project The foundational layer of free software - compilers, shells, and core utilities that power every Linux system. We believe firmly in the mission of the GNU ecosystem, and will give back to it at every opportunity. Foundation
LibreOffice Full office suite - Writer, Calc, Impress, and Base - with strong compatibility with .docx and .xlsx formats. Maintained by the Document Foundation. Office
OpenERP / Odoo CE Modular open-source ERP covering accounting, inventory, CRM, project management, and more. Community Edition is fully open-source and self-hostable. ERP
Ubuntu LTS / CentOS / Arch Linux Linux distributions with decade-long support cycles and hardware compatibility back to before some of us were born OS
Nextcloud Self-hosted file sync, calendar, contacts, and collaboration - a (more-or-less...) drop-in replacement for Microsoft 365's data-sharing components, running on your own server (or spare computer). Collaboration
pfSense / OPNsense Open-source firewall and router platforms based on FreeBSD, offering enterprise-grade network security with full transparency and no licence fees. Security
Thunderbird Mozilla's open-source email client with strong calendar and contacts integration - a reliable Outlook replacement for organisations of any size. Email
